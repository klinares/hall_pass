# Photo credits

## Hunt scenes

Six of the eight hunt scenes are free-licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons. Each one was
downloaded at full size, resized to 2400 px wide, converted to WebP (85% quality) with all
EXIF/metadata removed, and saved in `images/scenes/`. Chippy and the mess drawings are placed
on top by the game.

## Other pictures

- `images/caught/*.webp` (the Evidence File photos of Chippy making messes) were made by the
  family with Google Gemini and split from one 2x2 image.
- `images/chippy.png` is a cut-out of the family's own photo of Chippy, cleaned up and upscaled.
- `images/mess/*.svg` (flour, toilet paper, chocolate chips, marshmallows, candy-cane wrappers,
  ornaments, sock, boot print) and the cartoon agent badges were drawn for this game.
- Fonts are listed in `fonts/FONTS.txt`. All sounds are synthesized in the browser; there are no
  sound files.
