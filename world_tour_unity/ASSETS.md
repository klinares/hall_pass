# Third-party assets (world / 3D game)

Every asset below is CC0 (public domain) and owner-approved. Each row quotes the licence line and names the file it comes from in the downloaded pack, except the dog, whose licence could not be machine-verified (see its row). Only the files listed were
imported, under `Assets/World/Art/<pack>/`; each KayKit folder also keeps its own `LICENSE.txt`. Nothing is downloaded at
runtime. Recoloured copies of the palette textures (the pop outfit, the crew looks, blossom trees) are generated by
`Assets/Editor/WorldArtBuilder.cs` and `PlaceCoffeeBuilder.cs` into `Assets/World/Textures/Art/`.

Processing: the five clips were trimmed, made mono, normalised and re-encoded with ffmpeg (libvorbis); nothing else is imported from those packs.
The clips are kept out of `Assets/Resources` on purpose: the 2D `tour/` build includes every Resources folder, so they sit in `Assets/World/Audio/Clips/` and are reached through the `WorldAudioBank` scene (`WorldAudioClips`), which only `BuildWeb.BuildWorld` puts in the build.
No other sound is a file. The birds, the footsteps (per ground), the applause, the crowd murmur and the city hum are synthesised in code (`WorldSynth`, `WorldAudio.MakeCityHum`); the 2D game's own sounds are synthesised too (`Synth`). Four OpenGameArt clips that were imported earlier in 8.3 (footsteps, birds, applause, crowd murmur) were removed because their licence could not be machine-verified.

Notes
- The Adventurers FBX files are about 19 MB each in the project (they carry far more than the body parts we keep). Only the
  body meshes the prefabs reference, and the animation clips the controller references, go into the web build.
- Models are imported with FBX materials off; the project assigns its own URP Lit materials (palette textures at 256 to 512 px).
- 2026-11-24 (8.3 builder A, wardrobe): no new imports. The outfit and hair colours are 256 px recolours of the KayKit Adventurers palette texture (`Assets/World/Textures/Art/wd_*.png`, made by `Assets/Editor/WardrobeBuilder.cs` the same way as the Star's own look); the guitar, Riff's accessories and the wardrobe trunk are built at run time from Unity's built-in cube, sphere and cylinder and three small generated meshes (`MeshKit`). Nothing downloaded.
