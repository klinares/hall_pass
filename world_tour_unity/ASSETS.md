# Third-party assets (world / 3D game)

Every asset below is CC0 (public domain), owner-approved, and was licence-checked before import. Only the files listed were
imported, under `Assets/World/Art/<pack>/`; each KayKit folder also keeps its own `LICENSE.txt`. Nothing is downloaded at
runtime. Recoloured copies of the palette textures (the pop outfit, the crew looks, blossom trees) are generated by
`Assets/Editor/WorldArtBuilder.cs` and `PlaceCoffeeBuilder.cs` into `Assets/World/Textures/Art/`.

Notes
- The Adventurers FBX files are about 19 MB each in the project (they carry far more than the body parts we keep). Only the
  body meshes the prefabs reference, and the animation clips the controller references, go into the web build.
- Models are imported with FBX materials off; the project assigns its own URP Lit materials (palette textures at 256 to 512 px).
